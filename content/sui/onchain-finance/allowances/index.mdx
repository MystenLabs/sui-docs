Allowances let an address authorize another address to spend from its [address balance](/onchain-finance/asset-custody/address-balances) within limits it sets, and revoke that permission at any time. Typical uses include subscriptions and agent payments.

## How do allowances differ from sending funds to another wallet?

Funds stay in the funder's balance until a spend succeeds, so an allowance is a permission, not a deposit.

## What kinds of allowances are there?

A direct allowance names a spender that can spend within the limits with its own signature, without further approval from the funder. An app-bound allowance routes every spend through the Move module of a specific app, which can also change the spender. Both kinds are shared objects that the funder revokes with a capability only the funder holds.
