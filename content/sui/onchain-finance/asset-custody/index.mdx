Asset custody on Sui covers how applications and users hold and move assets. Address aliases let multiple keys act as 1 Sui address, so you can rotate keys without migrating assets. Address balances track fungible assets with a single per-address value instead of coin objects. Fiat on-ramps and off-ramps convert between fiat currency and Sui-native tokens through third-party providers. Wallets cover how Sui wallets work and the wallet types available, including Slush, self-custodial, and zkLogin wallets.
