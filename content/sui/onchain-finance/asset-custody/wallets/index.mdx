## What does a Sui wallet do?

A Sui wallet manages the credentials that prove ownership of your onchain assets and signs the transactions you submit to the network. How those credentials are held varies by wallet type: self-custodial wallets keep a private key, while zkLogin wallets derive authority differently. This section covers how wallets work, the wallet types available on Sui (including Slush, self-custodial wallets, and zkLogin wallets), how to integrate wallets into your app, and how SuiLink connects your wallet across apps.
