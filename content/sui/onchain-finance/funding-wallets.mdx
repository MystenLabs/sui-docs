Before users or agents can transact on Sui, their wallets need tokens. A new wallet that holds nothing cannot pay gas, so it cannot send any transaction until it is funded. Either fund the wallet directly using one of the methods on this page, or have someone else pay gas for its transactions. For agents, let the agent spend through an allowance or escrow grant, and cover gas with [sponsored transactions](/develop/transaction-payment/sponsor-txn), [gasless stablecoin transfers](/develop/transaction-payment/gasless-stablecoin-transfers), or a small SUI balance. See [How do I fund an AI agent's wallet?](#how-do-i-fund-an-ai-agents-wallet).

## Which funding method should I use?

- **Exchange withdrawal:** Use when users already hold SUI on a centralized exchange. It needs no integration in your app, so it is the first path to support.
- **Cross-chain bridge:** Use when users hold assets on Ethereum, Solana, or other blockchains.
- **Testnet and Devnet faucets:** Use for development and testing only. Faucet tokens have no monetary value.

## How do I withdraw SUI from an exchange?

Users who already hold SUI on a centralized exchange can withdraw to their Sui wallet address:

1. Copy the Sui address from the wallet.
2. In the exchange, initiate a withdrawal for SUI on the **Sui network**.
3. Paste the Sui address as the withdrawal destination, and check that it matches the address in the wallet before confirming.
4. The SUI arrives in the wallet after the exchange processes the withdrawal. Confirm the wallet balance shows the withdrawn SUI.

## How do I bridge tokens from other chains to Sui?

To move assets from Ethereum, Solana, or other blockchains to Sui, use a cross-chain bridge:

- [Wormhole](https://wormhole.com/): Supports bridging tokens between Sui and multiple chains including Ethereum, Solana, and others.

- [Sui Bridge](https://bridge.sui.io/): Native bridge between Sui and Ethereum.

Bridged assets arrive as wrapped tokens on Sui. The wrapped token's coin type address differs from the native token's address on the source chain.

## How do I get SUI on Testnet and Devnet?

For development and testing on Testnet and Devnet, see [Get SUI from Faucet](/getting-started/onboarding/get-coins) for CLI and programmatic faucet access.

Faucet tokens have no monetary value and cannot be transferred to Mainnet.

## How do I fund an AI agent's wallet?

Do not send an AI agent the money it spends. Keep the funds with the person or service that pays for the agent, and authorize the agent's address to spend within limits. Use an [allowance](/onchain-finance/allowances) on the funder's address balance, or an [escrow grant](/onchain-finance/agentic-payments/agent-wallets#fund-the-budget-not-the-agent) from a payment service, where the owner of the escrow sets the limits. A leaked agent key then exposes only the remaining authorized budget, and the funder or owner can revoke at any time.

The agent still needs a way to pay gas. Keep a small SUI balance at the agent's address, or use a [sponsor](/develop/transaction-payment/sponsor-txn) so the agent never holds SUI. See [Setting Up Agent Wallets and Signers](/onchain-finance/agentic-payments/agent-wallets#funding-an-agent) for gas options, funding, and Testnet faucets for SUI and USDC.
