The supply referral system allows users to earn a portion of the protocol fees generated by liquidity suppliers they refer. When a supplier deposits assets using your referral ID, you earn fees proportional to interest paid over time.

## How it works

1. **Create a referral**: Mint a `SupplyReferral` object for a specific margin pool with `mint_supply_referral`
2. **Referral ID**: Suppliers include your referral ID when supplying liquidity with `supply`. The referral ID is optional; suppliers can supply without one, in which case all protocol fees from that position go to the protocol
3. **Earn fees**: As referred suppliers earn interest, a portion of protocol fees accrues to your referral
4. **Claim fees**: Withdraw accumulated referral fees at any time with `withdraw_referral_fees`

The referral system uses a shares-based tracking mechanism. When a supplier deposits with your referral:
- Your referral's shares increase proportionally to the supplier's position
- As interest accrues and protocol fees are collected, your claimable fees (accumulated referral fees you have not yet withdrawn) grow
- When a supplier withdraws, your referral's shares decrease accordingly

## Fee distribution

The referral spread parameter determines what portion of protocol fees goes to referrers. The referral spread is configured per margin pool rather than network-wide, so it can differ between pools; 20% is a typical value.

```text
referrer_fees  = protocol_fees × referral_spread
protocol_share = protocol_fees × (1 − referral_spread)
```

In this formula, `protocol_fees` is the total protocol fees from a referred supplier's position before the split. `referrer_fees` is the portion the referrer earns, and `protocol_share` is the portion the protocol keeps.

For example:

- **Without referral**: 100% of protocol fees go to the protocol
- **With referral**: 80% to protocol, 20% to referrer (at 20% referral spread)

You can view the referral spread for each margin pool in the [Contract Information](/onchain-finance/deepbook/deepbook-margin/contract-information).
