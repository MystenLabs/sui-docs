The supply referral system allows users to earn a portion of the protocol fees generated by liquidity suppliers they refer. When a supplier deposits assets using your referral ID, you earn fees proportional to interest paid over time.

## How it works

1. **Create a referral**: Mint a `SupplyReferral` object for a specific margin pool with `mint_supply_referral`
2. **Referral ID**: Suppliers include your referral ID when supplying liquidity with `supply`. The referral ID is optional; suppliers can supply without one, in which case the position's shares count toward the default referral described in [Fee distribution](#fee-distribution)
3. **Earn fees**: As referred suppliers earn interest, a portion of protocol fees accrues to your referral
4. **Claim fees**: Withdraw accumulated referral fees at any time with `withdraw_referral_fees`

The referral system uses a shares-based tracking mechanism. When a supplier deposits with your referral:
- Your referral's shares increase proportionally to the supplier's position
- As interest accrues and protocol fees are collected, your claimable fees (accumulated referral fees you have not yet withdrawn) grow
- When a supplier withdraws, your referral's shares decrease accordingly

## Fee distribution

There is no separate referral spread parameter. Each margin pool sets a protocol spread, capped at 20% by the contract, that determines what portion of borrower interest the pool keeps as protocol fees instead of paying out to suppliers. The Mainnet SUI margin pool, for example, uses a 20% protocol spread.

Each time fees accrue, the pool splits them pool-wide in fixed proportions:

- **Protocol:** 1/4 of the accrued fees
- **Maintainer:** 1/4 of the accrued fees
- **Referrals:** the remaining 1/2, divided among referrals in proportion to the supply shares each one referred

The pool converts the referral half into a fees-per-share increase over its total supply shares, so a referral's claimable fees grow with the shares it referred relative to all shares in the pool. Shares supplied without a referral ID count toward a default referral (address `0x0`) whose fees only the admin can withdraw with `admin_withdraw_default_referral_fees`. If the pool has no supply shares when fees accrue, the referral half goes to the maintainer and the protocol instead.

You can view the protocol spread for each margin pool in the [Contract Information](/onchain-finance/deepbook/deepbook-margin/contract-information).
